# Alpha 32 neutral Home styling

Alpha 32 removes the colored metric-card variants introduced in Alpha 31 and
restores the compact schema-level statistics requested for Data Entry and
Builder.

## General statistics

The general Data Entry and Builder totals remain separate statistic cards. All
cards now share the same neutral translucent white surface, grey border, and
restrained shadow. Their hierarchy remains label first and number second, but
no card is assigned a lavender, green, amber, or blue theme.

## Schema statistics

Statistics belonging to the selected schema are compact tags beside the schema
title, as they were before the metric-card pass. They are not repeated as large
cards above the table. Data charts and history, and Builder history, therefore
begin immediately below the schema heading.

## Additional polish

- Schema panels use smoother corners and clipped internal surfaces.
- Panel headings have a subtle neutral fade and slimmer marker.
- The schema rail receives clearer keyboard focus without a colored outline.
- Selected tabs retain a quiet white surface and neutral indicator.
- Narrow layouts wrap schema tags below the title instead of compressing them.

This pass changes presentation only. Tab behavior, chart configuration, history
navigation, and collapse behavior remain unchanged.
