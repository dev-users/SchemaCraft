Calendar arithmetic used by schemacraft_alerts.py

The Persian year-start calculation is a Python adaptation of jalCal(year,true)
from jalaali-js v1.2.8. Date comparison in live alerts and the read-only Entry tools calendar use this conversion;
this update does not rewrite existing stored dates or the app's date validation.
Source: https://github.com/jalaali/jalaali-js/tree/v1.2.8
Copyright (c) 2020 Behrang Norouzinia

Civil/tabular Hijri date arithmetic follows the to_jd formula of convertdate.
Source: https://github.com/fitnr/convertdate/blob/master/src/convertdate/islamic.py
